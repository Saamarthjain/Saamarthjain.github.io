Major Research Project
₿

Cryptocurrency: An Inevitable Change

Vivekananda Institute of Professional Studies · 2024–25

An in-depth academic research project examining cryptocurrency not merely as a financial instrument, but as a structural shift in how economies, institutions, and individuals interact with money. The research traced the evolution of digital currencies — from Bitcoin's genesis as a peer-to-peer payment system to the broader emergence of decentralised finance (DeFi), stablecoins, and blockchain-based governance. It critically evaluated the macroeconomic implications of crypto adoption, including its potential to challenge central banking models, reshape cross-border transactions, and drive financial inclusion in underbanked populations. Regulatory responses across major jurisdictions were assessed, with the paper arguing that regulatory clarity — rather than prohibition — is the cornerstone of sustainable crypto integration into mainstream financial systems.
